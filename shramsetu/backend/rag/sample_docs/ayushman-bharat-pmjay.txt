Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) — Health Insurance

Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the world's largest government-funded health insurance scheme providing coverage up to Rs 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation.

Key Features:
- Covers hospitalisation expenses including pre and post-hospitalisation for up to 15 days
- Covers 1,929 medical and surgical packages including surgery, day care, ICU
- Cashless and paperless access at empanelled public and private hospitals
- Portable across India — get treated at any empanelled hospital in any state
- No restriction on family size, age, or gender

Eligibility:
- Based on Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 data
- Families identified as deprived (D1-D7) and occupational categories
- Rural: families without adult male member (16-59 years), disabled members, landless labourers, manual scavengers
- Urban: street vendors, domestic workers, shop employees, tailors, rickshaw pullers
- Families with monthly income up to Rs 10,000
- Migrant workers are eligible if their family is in the SECC database

How to Check Eligibility:
1. Visit https://mera.pmjay.gov.in
2. Enter mobile number and OTP
3. Search by name, ration card number, or mobile number
4. Download Ayushman card from the portal

How to Use the Benefit:
1. Visit any empanelled hospital (public or private)
2. Show Ayushman card or provide details at the hospital help desk
3. Hospital verifies eligibility and processes cashless treatment
4. No upfront payment needed — insurance covers the bill

Empanelled Hospitals:
- Over 29,000 hospitals across India including government and private
- Find nearest hospital at https://hospitals.pmjay.gov.in
- Toll-free helpline: 14555 / 1800-111-565

Important Numbers:
- Helpline: 14555
- Website: https://pmjay.gov.in
- Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission: https://abdm.gov.in

Documents Required:
- Ayushman card (can be downloaded from portal)
- Aadhaar card for identity verification
- Any government-issued photo ID
