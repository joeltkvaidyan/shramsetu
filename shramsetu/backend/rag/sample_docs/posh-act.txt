TITLE: Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2025-02-01
---
The POSH Act, 2013 provides protection to women against sexual harassment at the workplace and establishes a redressal mechanism for complaints. It applies to all workplaces including organized and unorganized sectors.

KEY PROVISIONS:
- Definition of sexual harassment: Any unwelcome sexually determined behaviour including physical contact, sexual advances, demand for sexual favours, sexually coloured remarks, showing pornography, or any other unwelcome physical, verbal or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature.
- Internal Complaints Committee (ICC): Every employer with 10 or more workers must constitute an ICC to receive and redress complaints of sexual harassment. The ICC must have at least 4 members, with a senior woman employee as presiding officer.
- Local Complaints Committee (LCC): For workplaces with less than 10 workers or where the complaint is against the employer, the District Magistrate shall constitute an LCC.
- Complaint timeline: The complaint must be filed within 3 months from the date of incident.
- Interim relief: The ICC/LCC may recommend interim measures including transfer of the complainant or the respondent, or leave from work.
- Penalty: For non-compliance with the provisions of the Act, the employer may be fined up to Rs 50,000. Repeated non-compliance may result in doubling of the penalty and cancellation of licence.

MIGRANT WOMEN WORKERS:
- Migrant women workers have the same protection under the POSH Act as any other woman worker.
- The Act applies to all workplaces including construction sites, brick kilns, agricultural fields, domestic work, and factories.
- Women workers can approach the ICC at their workplace or the LCC at the district level.
- Language barriers should not prevent filing complaints. The complaint can be filed in any language the complainant is comfortable with.
- Employers of migrant women workers must ensure the ICC has members who can communicate in the local language.

REMEDIES:
- Service of the complainant shall not be terminated or subjected to any disadvantage.
- Transfer of the respondent to another workplace.
- Monetary compensation for the loss suffered.
- Criminal prosecution under Section 354 of the Indian Penal Code.
