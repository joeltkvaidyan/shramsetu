TITLE: Employees' Compensation Act, 1923 — Compensation for Work-Related Injuries and Death
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2025-03-01
---
The Employees' Compensation Act (formerly Workmen's Compensation Act) provides compensation to 
employees (or their dependents) who suffer injury, disability, or death arising out of and in 
the course of employment. This is a critical protection for migrant workers who often face 
hazardous working conditions.

Applicability:
- Applies to all employees, including migrant workers, in establishments covered under the Act.
- Covers factories, mines, plantations, construction sites, and other establishments as 
  notified by the government.
- Does NOT apply to employees covered under the ESI Act or the Armed Forces.

Compensation amounts:
- Death: Minimum compensation is the amount equal to 50% of the monthly wages of the deceased 
  employee multiplied by the relevant factor (based on age), or Rs 1,20,000, whichever is higher.
- Total disablement: The same amount as for death, reduced proportionally for partial disablement.
- Partial disablement: Compensation is paid as a percentage of the full disablement amount, 
  based on the percentage of disablement certified by a medical board.
- Time limit: Compensation must be claimed within 2 years from the date of accident (or death).

Important provisions for migrant workers:
- The employer is liable to pay compensation regardless of whether the worker is a local or 
  migrant.
- The compensation is in addition to any other remedy available under any other law.
- The employer cannot contract out of this liability (any agreement to the contrary is void).
- If the employer fails to pay compensation, the employee can file a claim with the 
  Commissioner for Employees' Compensation.

How to claim:
- File a claim with the Commissioner for Employees' Compensation in the area where the 
  accident occurred.
- Submit: medical certificate, proof of employment, details of the accident, and any other 
  relevant documents.
- The commissioner will investigate and determine the compensation amount.
- The compensation is payable within 30 days of the award.

Important contacts:
- District Labour Office.
- Commissioner for Employees' Compensation (appointed by the state government).
- Toll-free labour helpline: 14400.
