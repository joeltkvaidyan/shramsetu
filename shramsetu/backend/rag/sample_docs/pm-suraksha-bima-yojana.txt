TITLE: Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) — Accident Insurance
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Finance (Department of Financial Services), Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2025-01-10
---
PMSBY is a government-backed accident insurance scheme open to any Indian resident, including migrant
workers, aged 18 to 70 years with a bank account and Aadhaar-linked mobile number.

Premium: Rs 20 per year (subject to periodic revision), auto-debited from the linked bank account
between 1 June and the enrolment window each year.

Coverage:
- Rs 2 lakh for accidental death.
- Rs 2 lakh for full and irrecoverable loss of both eyes or use of both hands/feet, or loss of one eye
  and one hand/foot.
- Rs 1 lakh for loss of use of one eye or one hand/foot.

How to enroll: Fill a simple one-page enrolment form at your bank branch, or enroll through net banking,
mobile banking apps, or the Jan Suraksha portal. Many banks allow auto-renewal each year unless the
worker opts out.

How to claim: The nominee or worker must contact the bank branch where the policy was enrolled, submit
a claim form along with a death certificate or disability certificate and FIR/post-mortem report (for
accidental death), and the bank forwards it to the insurance company for settlement.
