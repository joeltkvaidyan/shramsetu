TITLE: Rights and Protections for Power Sector Workers
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Power, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2025-02-15
---
This document covers the rights and protections available to workers in the power sector including thermal power plants, hydroelectric projects, renewable energy installations, and electrical distribution systems.

KEY RIGHTS:
1. Occupational Safety: Workers in power plants are entitled to safe working conditions under the Factories Act and OSH Code.
2. Hazardous Work: Workers handling high voltage, chemical plants, or boiler operations must receive special safety training.
3. PPE: Employers must provide personal protective equipment including helmets, safety shoes, gloves, and eye protection.
4. Health Monitoring: Regular health check-ups for workers exposed to hazardous conditions.
5. Night Shift: Workers in power plants working night shifts must receive additional allowances.

SOCIAL SECURITY:
- EPF and ESIC coverage for all workers
- PM-SYM for contractual and temporary workers
- PM-SBY accident insurance
- Gratuity for workers who have completed 5 years of service
- Bonus payment under Payment of Bonus Act

MIGRANT WORKERS:
- Migrant workers in power plants are entitled to same protections as local workers
- Hostel and accommodation facilities must be provided
- Canteen facilities during shift hours
- Transport facilities for shift workers
- Their children are entitled to education under RTE Act

TRAINING AND SKILL DEVELOPMENT:
- Skill India/PMKVY programs for power sector workers
- National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) certifications
- On-the-job training for new recruits
- Safety training as mandatory requirement
