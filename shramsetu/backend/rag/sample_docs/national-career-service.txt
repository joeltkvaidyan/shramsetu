National Career Service (NCS) — Employment Exchange

NCS is a one-stop digital platform connecting job seekers with employers across India. It replaces the old employment exchange system with a modern online portal.

Key Features:
- Free online registration for job seekers
- Job matching based on skills, location, and preferences
- Employers post vacancies and search candidates
- Career counselling and guidance services
- Skill assessment and training recommendations
- Available in multiple languages

How to Register:
1. Visit https://www.ncs.gov.in
2. Click on "Job Seeker" and create account
3. Complete profile with education, skills, experience
4. Upload resume and certificates
5. Set job preferences (location, salary, job type)
6. Receive job alerts matching your profile

For Migrant Workers:
- Set preferred work location in any city/state
- Search for jobs in destination city before migrating
- Filter by occupation: construction, manufacturing, services
- Find employers looking for workers in specific trades
- Get notifications for local job fairs and recruitment drives

Benefits:
- Access to lakhs of job listings from registered employers
- Direct contact with employers (no middleman)
- Skill gap analysis and recommended training
- Resume builder and career guidance
- Government job notifications
- Job fair and campus recruitment alerts

Documents Required for Registration:
- Aadhaar card
- Mobile number and email
- Educational certificates
- Skill certificates (if any)
- Experience certificates (if any)
- Resume/CV

Employer Registration:
- Companies register at https://www.ncs.gov.in/employer
- Post job vacancies for free
- Search candidate database
- Schedule interviews through the portal

Helpline:
