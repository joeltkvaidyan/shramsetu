TITLE: Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2024-11-15
---
The BOCW Act, 1996 protects the rights of construction workers, including migrant construction workers,
across India. Every state runs a Building and Other Construction Workers' Welfare Board funded by a cess
collected from construction employers.

Registration: A construction worker who has worked at least 90 days in the preceding 12 months can
register with the state BOCW Welfare Board using proof of construction work, age proof, and address proof.
Registration is usually free or has a nominal fee, and must be renewed annually or as per state rules.

Benefits available to registered construction workers:
- Financial assistance for medical treatment for the worker and dependents.
- Maternity benefit for women construction workers.
- Financial assistance for the education of children.
- Pension after the age of 60.
- Financial assistance for tools required for construction work.
- Assistance for house construction/purchase in some states.
- Ex-gratia payment to the family in case of death or disability due to a construction accident.

Employer obligations: Employers of establishments with 10 or more construction workers must be
registered under the Act, must maintain safety measures at the worksite, and must pay the cess to the
welfare board (1% of construction cost in most states).

Migrant worker note: A worker who migrates to a different state for construction work can register
with the welfare board of the state where they are currently working; the registration is not restricted
to their home state.
