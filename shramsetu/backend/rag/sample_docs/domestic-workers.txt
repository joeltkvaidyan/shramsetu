TITLE: Rights and Protections for Domestic Workers in India
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2025-02-15
---
Domestic workers are persons employed in household work including cooking, cleaning, washing, ironing, childcare, elderly care, gardening, and similar tasks. While there is no specific comprehensive legislation for domestic workers in India, they are protected under various laws and schemes.

LEGAL PROTECTIONS:
- Minimum Wages Act: Domestic workers are entitled to minimum wages as fixed by respective State Governments.
- Payment of Wages Act: Wages must be paid on time without arbitrary deductions.
- Maternity Benefit Act: Women domestic workers who have worked for at least 80 days are entitled to maternity benefits.
- Sexual Harassment at Workplace Act (POSH): Domestic workers are covered under the POSH Act.
- Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act: Domestic workers forced to work under debt bondage are protected.
- Child Labour (Prohibition) Act: Employment of children below 14 years as domestic help is prohibited.

STATE-SPECIFIC LAWS:
Several states have enacted specific legislation for domestic workers:
- Kerala: Domestic Workers Welfare and Social Security Act, 2015
- Karnataka: Domestic Workers Welfare and Social Security Act, 2012
- Tamil Nadu: Tamil Nadu Domestic Workers Welfare and Social Security Act, 2012
- Andhra Pradesh: Andhra Pradesh Domestic Workers Welfare Fund Act, 2006
- Maharashtra: Unorganised Sector Workers Social Security Act, 2015
- Rajasthan: Unorganised Workers Social Security Act, 2010

SCHEMES AVAILABLE:
- e-Shram registration: Domestic workers can register on the e-Shram portal for social security.
- PM-SYM Pension: Rs 3,000/month pension after age 60.
- PM-SBY Insurance: Rs 2 lakh accident insurance.
- Ayushman Bharat: Rs 5 lakh health insurance.
- PM-KISAN: Rs 6,000/year for those with agricultural land.

RIGHTS OF DOMESTIC WORKERS:
- Right to minimum wages
- Right to timely payment of wages
- Right to safe working conditions
- Right to weekly rest (at least one day per week)
- Right to leave (paid annual leave as per state law)
- Right to maternity benefit
- Right against sexual harassment
- Right to social security
- Right to form associations and unions
- Right to be free from bonded labour

MIGRANT DOMESTIC WORKERS:
- Migrant domestic workers have the same rights as local domestic workers.
- Employers cannot pay less because the worker is from another state.
- Migrant domestic workers can register on e-Shram portal.
- Their social security benefits are portable across states.
