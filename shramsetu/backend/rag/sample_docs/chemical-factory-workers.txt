TITLE: Rights and Safety of Chemical Factory Workers
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Labour and Employment / Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2025-02-15
---
This document covers the rights, safety measures, and protections available to workers in chemical factories, pharmaceutical plants, and hazardous material handling facilities.

KEY SAFETY MEASURES:
1. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):
   - Respiratory protection (masks, respirators)
   - Chemical-resistant gloves and clothing
   - Safety goggles and face shields
   - Safety boots and helmets
   - All PPE provided free by employer

2. Ventilation:
   - Local exhaust ventilation for hazardous processes
   - General dilution ventilation
   - Regular monitoring of air quality

3. Training:
   - Safety induction training for all new workers
   - Hazard communication training (Safety Data Sheets)
   - Emergency response training
   - First aid training

4. Medical Surveillance:
   - Pre-employment medical examination
   - Periodic health check-ups (every 6-12 months)
   - Biological monitoring for exposure to chemicals
   - Record maintenance of health records

WORKERS RIGHTS:
1. Right to know about hazardous chemicals used in the workplace
2. Right to receive safety training in a language they understand
3. Right to refuse unsafe work
4. Right to access medical records
5. Right to compensation for occupational diseases
6. Right to safe working conditions
7. Right to form trade unions

OCCUPATIONAL DISEASES:
- Workers exposed to certain chemicals may develop occupational diseases
- Diseases include: skin disorders, respiratory problems, neurological effects
- Workers are entitled to compensation under the Employees Compensation Act
- Employers must maintain records of workplace exposures

MIGRANT CHEMICAL WORKERS:
- Migrant workers must receive safety training in their native language
- Employers cannot deny safety equipment because of the worker's origin
- Workers must be informed about risks in a language they understand
- Medical facilities must be accessible and available in their language

COMPLAINTS:
- Workers can file complaints with the Inspector-cum-Facilitator
- Complaints can be filed anonymously
- Penalties for non-compliance: Imprisonment up to 2 years or fine up to Rs 5 lakh
