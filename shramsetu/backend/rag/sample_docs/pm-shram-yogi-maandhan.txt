TITLE: Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-Dhan (PM-SYM) Pension Scheme
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2025-03-01
---
The Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-Dhan (PM-SYM) is a voluntary and contributory pension scheme for unorganised workers including street vendors, rickshaw pullers, domestic workers, and other self-employed workers with monthly income up to Rs 15,000.

KEY PROVISIONS:
- Eligibility: Any unorganised worker or self-employed person aged 18-40 years with monthly income up to Rs 15,000.
- Contribution: Monthly contribution based on age at entry. Ranges from Rs 55/month (age 18) to Rs 200/month (age 40).
- Government contribution: The Central Government contributes an equal amount to the subscriber's contribution.
- Pension amount: Assured monthly pension of Rs 3,000 after the age of 60.
- Family pension: After the subscriber's death, the spouse shall receive 50% of the pension amount (Rs 1,500/month) as family pension.
- Enrolment: Enrolment can be done through Common Service Centres (CSCs) or at the e-Shram portal.
- Documents required: Aadhaar card, bank account details, and savings bank account or Jan Dhan account.
- Exit: Subscriber can exit after 10 years of service and before 60 years of age. In such cases, the subscriber's contribution along with interest shall be returned.
- Death benefit: If the subscriber dies before 60 years of age, the spouse can continue the scheme or exit with accumulated contribution plus interest.

MIGRANT WORKERS:
- Migrant workers who are self-employed or working in the unorganised sector are eligible.
- They can enroll at any CSC in any state, not just their native state.
- Their pension will be credited to their bank account regardless of where they are working.
- If they move to a different state, they can continue the scheme without interruption.

ENROLMENT PROCESS:
1. Visit the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or e-Shram portal
2. Fill the enrolment form with Aadhaar and bank details
3. Pay the monthly contribution
4. Receive confirmation and scheme certificate
