TITLE: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) — Income Support for Farmers
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2025-04-10
---
PM-KISAN is a central sector scheme that provides income support of Rs 6,000 per year to 
farmer families, paid in three equal instalments of Rs 2,000 each. This scheme is relevant 
for migrant workers who are also farmers or who come from agricultural families.

Eligibility:
- All farmer families who own cultivable land.
- The benefit is per family, not per individual.
- Both the husband and wife can be beneficiaries if they have separate landholdings and 
  separate bank accounts (the benefit is tied to the bank account, not the person).

Who is NOT eligible:
- Institutional landholders (companies, trust, society, etc.).
- Former or current constitutional post holders and their families.
- Government employees and pensioners (except Class IV/Group D workers).
- Income tax payers.
- Professionals like doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants.

How to register:
- Visit the PM-KISAN portal: https://pmkisan.gov.in
- Click on "New Farmer Registration" and enter Aadhaar number, mobile number, and bank 
  account details.
- Alternatively, register through Common Service Centres (CSCs) or the nearest agriculture 
  office.
- Update land records through the "Updation of Land Records" section on the portal.

Payment:
- The instalment is directly credited to the farmer's bank account through Direct Benefit 
  Transfer (DBT).
- Check payment status on the PM-KISAN portal using the Aadhaar number or bank account number.

For migrant workers:
- A migrant worker who owns agricultural land in their home state can still register and 
  receive PM-KISAN benefits while working in another state.
- The benefit is not location-dependent; it depends on land ownership.
- Ensure your Aadhaar and bank account are linked for seamless payment.

Grievance redressal:
- Call the PM-KISAN helpline 155261 (toll-free).
- File complaints on the PM-KISAN portal under "Grievance" section.
- Approach the District Agriculture Officer for local issues.
