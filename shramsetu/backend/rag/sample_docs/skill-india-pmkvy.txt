Skill India — Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)

PMKVY is the flagship scheme of the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship providing free skill training to Indian youth including migrant workers.

Key Features:
- Free skill training (30-600 hours) in over 400 job roles
- Training at Skill India Centres and empanelled training partners
- Government pays full training cost — no fee for candidates
- Stipend of Rs 8-10 per hour during training
- Certification by National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC)
- Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) for experienced workers

Eligibility:
- Indian citizens aged 15-45 years
- School/college dropouts and unemployed youth
- Migrant workers seeking to upgrade skills
- Workers with informal training seeking formal certification
- Preference to BPL (Below Poverty Line) candidates

Job Roles Available:
- Construction: Mason, Carpenter, Plumber, Electrician, Welder
- Services: Beauty & Wellness, Healthcare, Tourism & Hospitality
- Manufacturing: Assembly, Quality Control, Maintenance
- Agriculture: Organic farming, Food processing
- IT/ITeS: BPO, Data Entry, Web Design

How to Register:
1. Visit https://www.pmkvyofficial.org
2. Or visit nearest Skill India Centre / CSC
3. Choose training programme and job role
4. Provide Aadhaar card and bank account details
5. Attend training and appear for assessment
6. Receive NSDC certificate on passing

Benefits After Training:
- NSDC-certified skill certificate (nationally and internationally recognised)
- Placement assistance through training partners
- Entrepreneurship support through Mudra loan scheme
- Higher employability and earning potential
- Credit for Prior Learning (RPL) certification
