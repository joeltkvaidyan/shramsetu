TITLE: Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PM-JJBY)
DEPARTMENT: Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2025-03-01
---
The Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PM-JJBY) is a one-year term life insurance scheme renewable from year to year. It offers a life cover of Rs 2 lakh to the nominees at a nominal premium of Rs 436 per annum (approximately Rs 1.2 per day).

KEY PROVISIONS:
- Eligibility: Any individual aged 18-50 years having a savings bank account and who has given consent for auto-debit.
- Premium: Rs 436 per annum (including service tax).
- Coverage: Rs 2 lakh in case of death of the insured due to any reason.
- Nomination: The amount is paid to the nominee(s) registered for the bank account.
- Auto-debit: Premium is auto-debited from the savings bank account on or before the due date.
- Renewal: The scheme is renewable on 31st May every year. If the premium is not paid by the due date, the coverage lapses.
- Exit: Subscriber can exit the scheme at any time. If no claim has been filed, the premium for the current year is not refunded.
- Multiple accounts: If a person has more than one savings bank account linked to the same Aadhaar, the cover will be available only in one bank account.

MIGRANT WORKERS:
- Migrant workers with a savings bank account in any bank can enroll in PM-JJBY.
- They can enroll at any bank branch, not just the branch where they have an account.
- Premium is auto-debited from the bank account, so they don't need to visit the bank annually.
- The Rs 2 lakh benefit is paid to the nominee regardless of where the worker was employed.
- Migrant workers can nominate their family members living in their native state.

HOW TO ENROLL:
1. Visit your bank branch or net banking portal.
2. Fill the enrollment form (available at the branch or online).
3. Provide Aadhaar and bank account details.
4. Give consent for auto-debit of Rs 436 per year.
5. The cover starts from the date of enrollment.

CLAIM PROCESS:
1. Inform the bank about the death of the subscriber.
2. Submit the death certificate and claim form to the bank.
3. The bank processes the claim and transfers Rs 2 lakh to the nominee's bank account.
4. The claim is usually processed within 30 days.
