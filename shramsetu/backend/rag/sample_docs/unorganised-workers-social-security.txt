TITLE: Unorganised Workers' Social Security Act, 2008 — Social Security for Informal Workers
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2024-11-20
---
The Unorganised Workers' Social Security Act, 2008 provides a framework for social security 
benefits to workers in the unorganised sector, which includes the majority of India's workforce 
— approximately 93% of the total workforce. This is particularly important for migrant workers 
who often work in the informal sector without any social security coverage.

Definition of unorganised worker:
- A worker who is a home-based worker, self-employed worker, or wage worker in the unorganised 
  sector.
- A worker in the organised sector who is not covered under any of the social security schemes 
  (EPF, ESI, pension schemes).

Social security schemes under the Act:
- PM Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY): Accident insurance of Rs 2 lakh for accidental death or 
  full disability, Rs 1 lakh for partial disability. Premium: Rs 20 per year.
- PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY): Life insurance of Rs 2 lakh in case of death from 
  any cause. Premium: Rs 432 per year.
- PM Garib Kalyan Yojana: Free foodgrains for eligible families.
- National Pension System for Traders and Self-Employed Persons (NPS): Pension scheme with 
  government co-contribution.
- Atal Pension Yojana: Pension scheme for workers in the unorganised sector.
- PM Kisan Samman Nidhi: Income support of Rs 6,000 per year in three instalments for 
  farmer families.

How to register:
- Register on the e-Shram portal (eshram.gov.in) or visit a Common Service Centre (CSC).
- Registration requires: Aadhaar number, bank account details, mobile number linked to Aadhaar.
- Registration is free of cost.
- After registration, the worker receives a UAN (Universal Account Number) card.

Benefits of registration:
- Access to all government welfare schemes through a single platform.
- Automatic eligibility check for schemes based on the worker's profile.
- Accident insurance cover linked to the e-Shram card.
- Portability of benefits across states for migrant workers.

Grievance redressal:
- Call the e-Shram helpline 14434 (toll-free).
- File complaints on the e-Shram portal.
- Approach the District Labour Office for assistance.
