TITLE: Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) — Retirement Savings for Organised Workers
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2025-04-15
---
The Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) is a retirement savings scheme managed by the Employees' 
Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO). It provides a corpus of savings to employees for their 
retirement, and also covers medical, housing, and education needs.

Applicability:
- Applies to every factory and establishment with 20 or more employees.
- Both employer and employee contribute 12% of basic wages + dearness allowance to the EPF 
  account.
- The employee's 12% goes entirely to the EPF account.
- The employer's 12% is split: 8.33% to EPS (pension) and 3.67% to EPF.

Benefits:
- Accumulated balance: The total EPF balance (employee + employer contributions + interest) 
  is paid to the employee at the time of retirement, resignation, or after 2 months of 
  unemployment.
- Interest: EPF earns a tax-free interest rate declared annually by the government (currently 
  around 8.15% per annum for FY 2023-24).
- Partial withdrawal: Employees can withdraw partially for specific purposes:
  - Housing: Up to 90% of EPF balance after 5 years of service for purchase/construction of 
    a house.
  - Education: Up to 50% for higher education of self or children after 7 years of service.
  - Medical: Up to 6 times of monthly wages for medical treatment of self, spouse, children, 
    or dependent parents.
  - Marriage: Up to 50% for marriage of self or children after 7 years of service.
- Pension: EPS provides a monthly pension after age 58, based on the pensionable salary and 
  pensionable service.

Universal Account Number (UAN):
- Every EPF member is assigned a UAN, which remains the same throughout their career.
- The UAN can be used to track EPF balance, transfer contributions between employers, and 
  claim benefits online.
- Activate UAN at https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in

For migrant workers:
- When a worker changes jobs, they can transfer their EPF balance to the new employer using 
  the UAN.
- If there is a gap of more than 2 months between jobs, the worker can withdraw the EPF 
  balance.
- The UAN is portable across India; the worker does not need a new account for each employer.
- Ensure that the Aadhaar and bank account are linked to the UAN for online transfers and 
  withdrawals.

How to check EPF balance:
- Use the EPFO Unified Portal: https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in
- Use the UMANG app for mobile access.
- SMS: Send "EPFOHO UAN" to 7738299899.
- Missed call: Give a missed call to 011-22901406 from the registered mobile number.
