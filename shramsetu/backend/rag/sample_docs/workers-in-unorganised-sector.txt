TITLE: Rights and Social Security for Unorganised Workers
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2025-03-01
---
Unorganised workers constitute over 90% of India's workforce. This document provides comprehensive information about the rights, social security schemes, and protections available to unorganised workers including migrant workers, gig workers, and platform workers.

WHO ARE UNORGISED WORKERS:
- Workers in the unorganised sector include self-employed workers, home-based workers, and wage workers without formal employment contracts.
- Examples: street vendors, domestic workers, construction workers, agricultural labourers, rickshaw pullers, auto drivers, tailors, barbers, vendors, plumbers, electricians, and similar occupations.

KEY RIGHTS:
1. Minimum Wages: Unorganised workers are entitled to minimum wages as fixed by State Governments.
2. Timely Payment: Wages must be paid on time without arbitrary deductions.
3. Safe Working Conditions: Employers must provide safe working environment.
4. Social Security: Access to PM-SYM, PM-SBY, PM-JJBY, and Ayushman Bharat.
5. Skill Development: Access to PMKVY and other skill development programmes.
6. Credit Support: Access to MUDRA loans and PM-SVANidhi for vendors.
7. Housing: Access to PM Awas Yojana.
8. Education: Free education for children under RTE Act.
9. Health: Free health services at government hospitals and PHCs.

SOCIAL SECURITY SCHEMES:
1. e-Shram Portal: Registration for all unorganised workers
2. PM-SYM: Rs 3,000/month pension after age 60
3. PM-SBY: Rs 2 lakh accident insurance
4. PM-JJBY: Rs 2 lakh life insurance
5. Ayushman Bharat: Rs 5 lakh health insurance
6. PM-SVANidhi: Collateral-free loans for street vendors
7. PM-KISAN: Rs 6,000/year for farmers

MIGRANT UNORGANISED WORKERS:
- Migrant workers from unorganised sector have all the rights listed above
- e-Shram registration is portable across states
- PM-SYM, PM-SBY, PM-JJBY are available regardless of location
- Benefits are credited to bank accounts, making them location-independent
- Workers can register at any CSC or online portal

SKILL DEVELOPMENT:
- Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY): Free skill training
- Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL): Certification for existing skills
- Entrepreneurship development through PMEGP
- Digital literacy through PMGDISHA

CREDIT AND FINANCIAL SUPPORT:
- Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY): Loans up to Rs 10 lakh
- PM-SVANidhi: Collateral-free loans for street vendors (up to Rs 50,000)
- Stand-Up India: Loans for women and SC/ST entrepreneurs
- PMEGP: Credit-linked subsidy for micro-enterprises
