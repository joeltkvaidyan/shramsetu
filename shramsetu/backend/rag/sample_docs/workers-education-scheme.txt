TITLE: National Workers' Education Scheme — Skill Development and Training for Workers
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2024-10-15
---
The National Workers' Education Scheme is implemented through the Workers' Education Boards 
(WEB) to provide education, training, and skill development to workers, particularly those in 
the unorganised sector and migrant workers. The scheme aims to empower workers with knowledge 
of their rights, welfare schemes, and skills for better employment.

Key components:
- Residential training programmes for workers on topics such as labour laws, trade union 
  rights, welfare schemes, health and safety, and financial literacy.
- Non-residential awareness camps at the workplace or community level.
- Training for new entrants to the workforce, including young migrants.
- Orientation programmes for trade union functionaries and worker representatives.

Training areas:
- Knowledge of labour laws and workers' rights.
- Awareness of government welfare schemes (EPF, ESI, e-Shram, ration card, etc.).
- Health and safety at the workplace.
- Financial literacy and digital payment skills.
- Skill development for better employment opportunities.
- Entrepreneurship development through PMEGP and other schemes.

How to participate:
- Contact the nearest Workers' Education Board office in the state.
- Training programmes are usually free of cost for workers.
- Workers can also approach the District Labour Office for information about upcoming 
  training programmes.
- Many training programmes are conducted in local languages.

Benefits for migrant workers:
- Understand their rights and entitlements in the state where they are working.
- Learn about local welfare schemes and how to access them.
- Gain new skills for better employment opportunities.
- Build networks with other workers and trade unions.

Contact:
- Workers' Education Board offices are located in all state capitals and major cities.
- Call the labour helpline 14400 for information about training programmes.
- Visit the Ministry of Labour website: https://labour.gov.in
