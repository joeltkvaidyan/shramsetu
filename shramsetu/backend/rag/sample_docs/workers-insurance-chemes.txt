TITLE: Comprehensive Insurance and Pension Schemes for Workers in India
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Labour and Employment / Ministry of Finance, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2025-03-01
---
This document provides a comprehensive overview of all insurance and pension schemes available to workers in India, especially unorganised and migrant workers.

LIFE INSURANCE SCHEMES:
1. Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PM-JJBY):
   - Coverage: Rs 2 lakh
   - Premium: Rs 436/year (auto-debited from bank account)
   - Eligibility: 18-50 years, savings bank account
   - Claim: Death benefit paid to nominee within 30 days

2. Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PM-SBY):
   - Coverage: Rs 2 lakh (death/total disability), Rs 1 lakh (partial disability)
   - Premium: Rs 20/year (auto-debited)
   - Eligibility: 10-70 years, savings bank account
   - Claim: Accidental death or disability benefit

3. Aam Aadmi Bima Yojana (AABY):
   - Coverage: Rs 30,000 (natural death), Rs 75,000 (accidental death/total disability), Rs 37,500 (partial disability)
   - Premium: Rs 200/year (shared: Rs 100 government, Rs 100 subscriber)
   - Eligibility: 18-59 years, BPL families

PENSION SCHEMES:
1. Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-Dhan (PM-SYM):
   - Pension: Rs 3,000/month after age 60
   - Contribution: Rs 55-200/month (age-based)
   - Government matches the contribution
   - Eligibility: Unorganised workers, monthly income up to Rs 15,000

2. National Pension System (NPS):
   - Market-linked pension with government contribution
   - Minimum contribution: Rs 1,000/year
   - Government contributes 50% (up to Rs 1,000) for unorganised workers
   - Eligibility: 18-65 years

3. Atal Pension Yojana (APY):
   - Guaranteed pension: Rs 1,000-5,000/month after age 60
   - Government co-contribution: 50% for eligible subscribers
   - Eligibility: 18-40 years, bank account, not covered under statutory social security

HEALTH INSURANCE:
1. Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY):
   - Coverage: Rs 5 lakh per family per year for hospitalisation
   - Eligibility: BPL families identified through SECC data
   - 1,949 packages including surgery, medical, day care
   - Cashless and paperless treatment at empanelled hospitals

2. ESIC (Employees State Insurance Corporation):
   - Medical benefit: Complete medical care for self and family
   - Sickness benefit: 70% of daily wages for up to 91 days
   - Maternity benefit: 100% of wages for 26 weeks
   - Disablement benefit: Compensation for employment injury
   - Dependent benefit: Pension to dependents of deceased worker
   - Eligibility: Employees earning up to Rs 21,000/month

MIGRANT WORKERS:
- All schemes listed above are portable across states
- Migrant workers can enroll from any location
- Benefits are credited directly to bank accounts
- e-Shram registration links workers to all available schemes
- PM-SYM, PM-SBY, PM-JJBY are available regardless of location

HOW TO ENROLL:
- e-Shram portal: eshram.gov.in
- Common Service Centres (CSCs)
- Bank branches
- Post offices
