TITLE: Inter-State Migrant Workmen (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2024-08-20
---
The Inter-State Migrant Workmen Act, 1979 (now largely subsumed into the Occupational Safety, Health
and Working Conditions Code, 2020) protects workers who are recruited in one state to work in an
establishment in another state, typically through a contractor.

Key protections for inter-state migrant workers:
- The contractor recruiting a worker across state lines must be licensed by the government.
- Workers must be issued a passbook containing details of their identity, wage rate, and employer.
- Equal wages must be paid to inter-state migrant workers as to other workers doing the same or similar
  work in that establishment; wages cannot be lower merely because the worker is a migrant.
- Displacement allowance equal to 50% of monthly wages (or Rs 75, whichever is higher, under the older
  Act) must be paid at the time of recruitment.
- Journey allowance (to and from the home state) must be paid by the employer or contractor.
- Suitable residential accommodation, free medical facilities, and protective clothing must be provided
  during the period of employment.
- In case of injury or death during employment, the worker or their family is entitled to compensation
  under applicable labour laws.

Where to complain: A worker who is denied these protections can file a complaint with the Labour
Commissioner's office of the state where they are working, or use the Government/NGO complaint portal
within ShramSetu once available.
