TITLE: Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2025-03-01
---
MGNREGA is a flagship programme of the Government of India that provides legal guarantee for 100 days of wage employment in a financial year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work. It is the world's largest public works programme.

KEY PROVISIONS:
1. Legal Entitlement: Every rural household has a legal right to 100 days of employment per year.
2. Work Within 15 Days: Employment must be provided within 15 days of application.
3. Unemployment Allowance: If employment is not provided within 15 days, the applicant is entitled to unemployment allowance.
4. Minimum Wages: Wages are paid at the state's minimum wage rate for agricultural labourers.
5. Payment Through Bank: Wages are paid through bank/post office accounts, not in cash.
6. Work Demand: Any member of a rural household can demand work by submitting a written application to the Gram Panchayat.
7. Work Within 5 km: Work must be provided within 5 km of the applicant's residence. If not, 10% additional wages are paid.
8. Women Participation: At least one-third of beneficiaries must be women. Women are paid equal wages.
9. Time Limit: Work must be completed within the specified time limit. If not, the worker is entitled to compensation.

ELIGIBILITY:
- Any adult member of a rural household who volunteers for unskilled manual work
- Job card must be registered with the Gram Panchayat
- Aadhaar-linked bank account for wage payment

WORK TYPES:
- Water conservation and harvesting
- Drought proofing (including afforestation)
- Land development
- Flood control and protection
- Rural connectivity (roads, paths)
- Individual beneficiary schemes
- Any other work notified by the state government

MIGRANT WORKERS:
- MGNREGA job card is linked to the worker's native village
- Migrant workers can use their job card in their home state
- Some states have extended MGNREGA to urban areas (AMRUT, Smart Cities)
- If a migrant worker returns to their native village, they can resume MGNREGA work
- MGNREGA wages are credited to the bank account, making them portable

WAGE PAYMENT:
- Wages are credited to the bank/post office account on a weekly basis
- Maximum delay in wage payment: 15 days from completion of work
- If delayed, compensation is paid at 0.05% per day of delay
- In some states, minimum wage for agricultural labourers is higher than MGNREGA wage rate

GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL:
- Gram Panchayat-level complaint
- Block Development Officer
- District Programme Coordinator
- State-level monitoring committee
- National-level monitoring by MGNREGA Commissioner
