TITLE: Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 — Modern Labour Law Framework
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2025-06-15
---
The Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions (OSH) Code, 2020 consolidates and 
reforms 13 central labour laws, including the Factories Act, Mines Act, Inter-State Migrant 
Workmen Act, and Contract Labour Act. It is designed to provide a modern, unified framework for 
occupational safety, health, and working conditions for all workers, including migrant workers.

Key provisions for migrant workers:
1. Inter-state migrant workers: The code provides specific protections for workers who are 
   recruited in one state to work in another state. The principal employer must ensure:
   - Registration of the establishment with the relevant authority.
   - A passbook for each inter-state migrant worker containing details of identity, wages, 
     and working conditions.
   - Journey allowance for travel from the home state to the workplace.
   - Suitable residential accommodation during the period of employment.
   - Medical facilities at the workplace.
   - Displacement allowance at the time of recruitment.
2. Equal wages: Inter-state migrant workers must be paid the same wages as other workers doing 
   the same or similar work in the establishment.
3. Working hours: The code limits working hours to 8 hours per day and 48 hours per week, 
   with overtime paid at twice the ordinary rate.
4. Safety standards: The code mandates safety standards for all workplaces, including:
   - Provision of clean drinking water.
   - Adequate toilet and washing facilities.
   - Ventilation, lighting, and temperature control.
   - First-aid facilities and accident prevention measures.
   - Safety training for workers.
5. Annual health examinations: Workers exposed to occupational hazards must be provided with 
   annual health examinations at the employer's cost.

Registration and documentation:
- Every inter-state migrant worker must be registered by the principal employer within 30 days 
  of employment.
- The employer must issue a passbook to each worker containing:
  - Name and address of the worker.
  - Name and address of the employer.
  - Nature of work.
  - Wage rate and other terms of employment.
  - Period of employment.
- The passbook must be returned to the worker upon termination of employment.

Penalties for violations:
- Failure to register inter-state migrant workers: Fine up to Rs 3 lakh.
- Non-payment of displacement allowance: Fine up to Rs 1 lakh.
- Non-provision of residential accommodation: Fine up to Rs 2 lakh.
- Violation of safety standards: Fine up to Rs 5 lakh and/or imprisonment up to 2 years.

How to enforce rights:
- File a complaint with the Inspector-cum-Facilitator.
- Approach the Labour Court or Industrial Tribunal.
- Call the labour helpline 14400 for guidance.
- Use the SHRAM Suvidha Portal (shramsuvidha.gov.in) for online registration and compliance.
