TITLE: Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Yojana (PMGKY) — Food Security for Migrant Workers
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Finance / Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2025-03-15

The Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Yojana was launched to provide food security to migrant workers 
and other vulnerable populations, especially during economic disruptions. It operates through the 
public distribution system (PDS) and provides free foodgrains to those in need.

Key provisions for migrant workers:
- Free foodgrains: 5 kg of wheat per person per month and 1 kg of chana (chickpea) per person 
  per month, over and above the regular NFSA entitlement.
- Coverage: All migrants who are not covered under NFSA or state schemes (including those without 
  a ration card) are eligible through the "One Nation One Ration Card" system.
- Duration: Extended periodically by the government; currently operational through March 2025 
  with possible extensions.

How migrant workers benefit:
- Migrant workers who do not have a ration card can still access foodgrains at any FPS using 
  their Aadhaar card, through the ONORC facility.
- Those with ration cards from other states can draw their entitled quantity at any FPS in the 
  state where they are currently working.
- The additional free foodgrains under PMGKY are available to all NFSA beneficiaries regardless 
  of their location.

Registration requirements:
- Aadhaar card linked to a bank account and mobile number.
- Ration card (if available) from the home state.
- If no ration card: visit any FPS with Aadhaar and register through the ePoS device.

Grievance redressal:
- Call the toll-free PDS helpline 1967.
- File complaints on the state Food and Civil Supplies portal.
- Use the Mera Ration app (available on Google Play Store) to locate nearby FPS shops and 
  check entitlements.
