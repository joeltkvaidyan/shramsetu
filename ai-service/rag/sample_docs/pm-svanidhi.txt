TITLE: PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi (PM SVANidhi) — Credit Support for Street Vendors
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2025-05-01
SOURCE_URL: https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in
LAST_VERIFIED: needs review
STATUS: current
---
PM SVANidhi is a central sector scheme launched on June 1, 2020, to provide affordable working 
capital credit to street vendors who have been adversely affected by COVID-19 and other economic 
disruptions. Many migrant workers who become street vendors in cities benefit from this scheme.

Eligibility:
- Street vendors, hawkers, and footpath vendors who are selling goods from fixed or mobile 
  structures in urban areas.
- Vendors who possess a Certificate of Registration/Identity Card issued by the Urban Local 
  Body (ULB) or Town Vending Committee (TVC).
- Both existing vendors and new vendors who have been vending since before March 24, 2020.

Loan details:
- First loan: Up to Rs 10,000 as working capital loan, repayable within 12 months.
- Second loan: Up to Rs 20,000, repayable within 12 months, available after repaying the 
  first loan on time.
- Third loan: Up to Rs 50,000, repayable within 12 months, available after repaying the 
  second loan on time.
- Subsidy: 7% per annum interest subsidy is credited directly to the vendor's bank account 
  after repayment of the loan.

Digital transactions incentive:
- Vendors who make digital payments through UPI/digital modes during the loan period receive 
  up to Rs 100 per month as cashback, capped at Rs 1,200 per year.

How to apply:
- Visit the PM SVANidhi portal: https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in
- Apply online with Aadhaar card, bank account details, and ULB/TVC registration certificate.
- Alternatively, apply through Common Service Centres (CSCs) or bank branches.
- The loan is processed through banks, NBFCs, and MFIs partnered with the scheme.

Registration for street vendors:
- To get a ULB/TVC certificate, approach the local Urban Local Body or Town Vending Committee.
- Provide proof of vending (self-declaration or photographs), Aadhaar card, and address proof.
- The certificate is issued free of cost.
