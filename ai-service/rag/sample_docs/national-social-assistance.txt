The National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP) is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme providing social assistance to poor households in the case of old age, death of the breadwinner, and maternity. It is a flagship programme of the Ministry of Rural Development.

COMPONENTS:
1. Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS):
   - Pension: Rs 200-500/month depending on age (60-79 years: Rs 200, 80+ years: Rs 500).
   - Eligibility: BPL families, aged 60 years or above.
   - Central share: 100% of the pension amount.

2. Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (IGNWPS):
   - Pension: Rs 300-500/month (40-79 years: Rs 300, 80+ years: Rs 500).
   - Eligibility: BPL families, widow aged 40 years or above.
   - Central share: 100% of the pension amount.

3. Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme (IGNDPS):
   - Pension: Rs 300-500/month (18-79 years: Rs 300, 80+ years: Rs 500).
   - Eligibility: BPL families, persons with 80% or more disability aged 18 years or above.
   - Central share: 100% of the pension amount.

4. National Family Benefit Scheme (NFBS):
   - Benefit: Rs 20,000 one-time grant.
   - Eligibility: BPL families where the breadwinner (aged 18-59 years) dies.
   - The breadwinner must be the primary earner in the household.

5. Annapurna Scheme:
   - Benefit: 10 kg of food grains per month free of cost.
   - Eligibility: Senior citizens not covered under IGNOAPS.

MIGRANT WORKERS:
- Migrant workers from BPL families are eligible for NSAP benefits regardless of where they are living.
- They can apply for benefits at the local Panchayat or municipal body.
- The benefits are credited to the bank account, so they are portable.
- Old age pension and widow pension are available to migrant workers who meet the eligibility criteria.
- Disability pension is available to migrant workers with disabilities.

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit the local Panchayat or municipal body.
2. Fill the application form (available in local language).
3. Submit required documents (Aadhaar, BPL certificate, age proof, bank details).
4. The application is verified and approved by the Panchayat.
5. Benefits are credited to the bank account.
