The Employees' State Insurance (ESI) scheme provides comprehensive social security benefits to 
workers in the organized sector, including medical, sickness, maternity, disability, and 
dependents' benefits. It is managed by the Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC).

Eligibility:
- Any employee working in a factory or establishment with 10 or more employees, earning up to 
  Rs 21,000 per month (Rs 25,000 for persons with disabilities).
- Both employer and employee contribute: employer contributes 3.25% and employee contributes 
  0.75% of wages.
- Applicable across India in all states and union territories.

Benefits:
- Medical benefit: Free medical treatment for the insured person and their family at ESI 
  hospitals and dispensaries. This includes outpatient treatment, hospitalization, surgery, 
  and maternity care.
- Sickness benefit: 70% of average daily wages for up to 91 days in two consecutive benefit 
  periods, if the insured person is certified medically unfit.
- Maternity benefit: 100% of average daily wages for 26 weeks (extendable to 30 weeks for 
  certain categories) for confinement/miscarriage.
- Disablement benefit: 70% to 90% of average daily wages depending on the nature and extent 
  of disability, for both temporary and permanent disability.
- Dependants' benefit: 70% of average daily wages to dependents (spouse, children, dependent 
  parents) in case of death due to employment injury.
- confinement expense: Rs 7,000 or actual expense, whichever is less.
- Vocational rehabilitation: Training and financial assistance for disabled workers.

How to register:
- The employer is responsible for registering employees under ESI.
- Employees can check their ESI status using their insurance number at www.esic.gov.in.
- ESI card (Pehchan card) can be downloaded from the ESIC portal.

Important for migrant workers: If a worker moves to a new state, their ESI benefits continue 
as long as they are employed under an ESI-covered establishment. The ESI number is portable 
across India.
