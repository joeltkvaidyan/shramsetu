Fishermen and fisheries workers form a significant part of India's unorganised workforce. This document covers the rights, legal protections, and welfare schemes available to fishermen and fisheries workers.

KEY RIGHTS:
1. Minimum Wages: Fishermen workers are entitled to minimum wages as fixed by respective State Governments.
2. Safe Working Conditions: Employers must provide safe boats, equipment, and safety gear.
3. Insurance: Life and accident insurance for fishermen at sea.
4. Medical Facilities: Access to medical facilities at fishing harbours and landing centres.
5. Housing: Housing schemes for fishermen families.
6. Education: Free education for fishermen's children under RTE Act.

SOCIAL SECURITY SCHEMES:
1. National Fisheries Development Board (NFDB) schemes
2. PM-SYM: Rs 3,000/month pension after age 60
3. PM-SBY: Rs 2 lakh accident insurance
4. PM-JJBY: Rs 2 lakh life insurance
5. Ayushman Bharat: Rs 5 lakh health insurance
6. e-Shram Registration: For social security benefits

MIGRANT FISHERMEN:
- Migrant fishermen working in other states have the same rights as local fishermen
- Employers must provide safe accommodation and food during fishing season
- Insurance coverage applies regardless of where the work is performed
- Benefits are portable across states through e-Shram registration

FISHING VESSEL REGISTRATION:
- All fishing vessels must be registered with the relevant authorities
- Safety equipment must be installed on all vessels
- Crew members must be trained in safety procedures
- Emergency communication equipment must be available
