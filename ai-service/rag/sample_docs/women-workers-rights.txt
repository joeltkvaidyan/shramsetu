Women workers in India are protected under a comprehensive framework of labour laws and social security schemes. This document provides an overview of the key rights and protections available to women workers, including migrant women workers.

KEY RIGHTS:
1. Equal Remuneration: Equal pay for equal work under the Code on Wages, 2019 (in force from 21 November 2025; it replaced the Equal Remuneration Act, 1976).
2. Maternity Benefit: 26 weeks paid leave for first two children, 12 weeks for subsequent children, continuing under the Code on Social Security, 2020 (which replaced the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961).
3. Protection from Sexual Harassment: POSH Act, 2013 provides protection against sexual harassment at workplace.
4. Working Hours: Women cannot be required to work before 6 AM and after 7 PM without proper safety measures.
5. Night Shift: States that allow night shifts for women must ensure transportation, safety measures, and consent.
6. Creche: Establishments with 50+ workers must provide crèche facilities for children below 6 years.
7. No Dismissal During Maternity: No woman shall be dismissed during or on account of maternity leave.

MIGRANT WOMEN WORKERS:
- Migrant women workers have all the rights listed above regardless of their native state.
- Employers cannot discriminate against women workers from other states.
- Migrant women workers are covered under PM-SYM, PM-SBY, and Ayushman Bharat schemes.
- Women working in agriculture, construction, brick kilns, and domestic work have specific protections.
- Free education for children of migrant women workers under the Right to Education Act.

HEALTH AND SAFETY:
- Pregnant women cannot be asked to lift heavy loads or do hazardous work.
- Women workers in factories are entitled to separate washrooms and restrooms.
- Women working in mines are entitled to additional safety measures.
- Free health check-ups and medical facilities in factories with 50+ women workers.

SOCIAL SECURITY SCHEMES:
- e-Shram registration for social security.
- PM-SYM: Rs 3,000/month pension after age 60.
- PM-SBY: Rs 2 lakh accident insurance for Rs 20/year.
- PM-JJBY: Rs 2 lakh life insurance for Rs 436/year.
- Ayushman Bharat: Rs 5 lakh health insurance.
- PM-MATRU VANDANA YOJANA: Rs 5,000 cash incentive for first child.
- PM-UDAN: For safe transportation during pregnancy.
