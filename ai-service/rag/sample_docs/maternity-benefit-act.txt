TITLE: Maternity Benefit Act, 1961
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2025-03-01
SOURCE_URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2192463
LAST_VERIFIED: 2026-10-01
STATUS: superseded
---
The Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 protects the employment of women during the time of maternity and entitles them to a paid maternity benefit. The Act applies to all establishments employing 10 or more persons including factories, mines, plantations, shops and establishments.

KEY PROVISIONS:
- Eligibility: Woman who has worked in the establishment for at least 80 days in the 12 months preceding the expected date of delivery.
- Duration of maternity leave: 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children. 12 weeks for the third child onwards. 12 weeks for commissioning mothers and adopting mothers.
- Maternity benefit amount: Average daily wage for the period of actual absence from work (up to 26 weeks).
- Work from home: After the period of maternity leave, the employer may allow the woman to work from home if the nature of work permits it.
- Medical bonus: An additional medical bonus of up to Rs 1,000 is payable if the employer does not provide adequate maternity health services.
- Creche facility: Every establishment with 50 or more employees must provide creche facilities within prescribed distance. Woman is allowed 4 visits to the creche per day including during rest intervals.
- No dismissal: No woman shall be dismissed during or on account of absence due to maternity leave. Dismissal during this period is deemed to be unfair labour practice.
- Notice: Woman must give written notice to the employer at least 7 days before the date of delivery (or the date from which she will be absent).

MIGRANT WORKERS:
- Migrant women workers are equally entitled to maternity benefits under this Act regardless of their native state.
- The employer cannot deny maternity benefit because the worker is from another state.
- Women working in unorganised sector can also benefit through the e-Shram portal registration which links them to social security schemes including maternity benefits.

COMPLAINTS:
- A woman denied maternity benefit can file a complaint with the Inspector appointed under the Act.
- Complaint must be filed within one year from the date on which the maternity benefit was payable.
- Penalty for non-payment: Imprisonment up to one year or fine up to Rs 5,000 or both.

REPEALED — HISTORICAL INFORMATION ONLY: The Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 was repealed when the Code on Social Security, 2020 came into force on 21 November 2025. Everything above describes the law as it stood before that date; maternity benefit provisions continue under the Code on Social Security, 2020. See the "Code on Social Security, 2020" document.
