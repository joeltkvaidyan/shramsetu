This document covers the housing, sanitation, and basic amenities schemes available to workers, especially migrant and unorganised sector workers in India.

HOUSING SCHEMES:
1. Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY):
   - PMAY-Urban: Subsidy on home loans for urban poor
   - PMAY-Gramin: Financial assistance for rural housing (Rs 1.20-1.30 lakh)
   - Eligibility: BPL families, EWS, LIG families
   - Migrant workers with BPL status in native state can avail

2. EWS and LIG Housing:
   - 15-20% of houses in all housing projects reserved for EWS/LIG
   - Subsidised rates and easy EMI options

SANITATION SCHEMES:
1. Swachh Bharat Mission (SBM):
   - Free individual household toilets (IHHL) for BPL families
   - Rs 12,000 per toilet construction
   - Community and public toilets in urban areas
   - Solid waste management in cities

2. Swachh Vidyalaya Abhiyan:
   - Toilet facilities in all schools
   - Separate toilets for boys and girls
   - Hand washing facilities

WATER SUPPLY:
1. Jal Jeevan Mission:
   - Functional tap water connection to every rural household by 2024
   - 55 litres per capita per day
   - Quality testing facilities at district and state levels

2. National Rural Drinking Water Programme:
   - Piped water supply to villages
   - Water quality monitoring

ELECTRICITY:
1. Saubhagya Scheme:
   - Electricity connection to all unelectrified households
   - Free connections for BPL families
   - Migrant workers' temporary residences also covered

2. PM-KUSUM:
   - Solar power for agriculture
   - Benefits for rural and agricultural workers

MIGRANT WORKERS:
- Migrant workers living in construction worker colonies, brick kilns, or rented accommodation
- Access to community toilets under Swachh Bharat Mission
- Water supply through Jal Jeevan Mission in rural areas
- Housing schemes available in both native and working states
- e-Shram registration provides access to all welfare schemes
