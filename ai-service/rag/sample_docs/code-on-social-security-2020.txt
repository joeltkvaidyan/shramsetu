TITLE: Code on Social Security, 2020 — Current Law on Social Security, Compensation and Maternity Benefit
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2026-10-01
AUTHORITY: Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
SOURCE_URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2192463
LAST_VERIFIED: 2026-10-01
STATUS: current

The Code on Social Security, 2020 came into force on 21 November 2025 as one of the four Labour
Codes and is now the central law on social security for all workers, including migrant and
unorganised-sector workers. It consolidated several earlier social security laws — among them
the Employees' Compensation Act, 1923, the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 and the Unorganised
Workers' Social Security Act, 2008 (the complete list of subsumed laws: needs review).
(Source: PIB press release PRID 2192463; PIB Year End Review 2025, PRID 2209767.)

Key protections that continue under the Code:
- Maternity benefit: Women workers continue to be entitled to paid maternity leave — 26 weeks
  for the first two children and 12 weeks thereafter — and dismissal on account of maternity
  absence is prohibited. Because of the new uniform wage definition, the maternity benefit
  amount is designed to be higher than before. (Source: PIB Year End Review 2025, PRID 2209767.)
- Compensation for employment injury or death: If a worker suffers injury, disability or death
  arising out of employment, the employer's liability to pay compensation continues under the
  Code. Commute-related accidents are now treated as employment-related, making the employee
  eligible for compensation. (Source: PIB Year End Review 2025, PRID 2209767.)
- Social security for unorganised workers: Registration of unorganised workers (through the
  e-Shram portal, eshram.gov.in) and access to welfare schemes continue under the Code's
  framework for universal social security. The Code also extends coverage to gig workers and
  platform workers.
- The welfare schemes linked to e-Shram registration — accident insurance, life insurance,
  pension schemes such as PM-SYM — continue as central sector schemes.

How to use these rights:
- For e-Shram registration and scheme linkage: register at eshram.gov.in or visit a Common
  Service Centre (free of cost), or call the e-Shram helpline 14434.
- For maternity benefit or compensation disputes: file a complaint with the officer designated
  under the Code in your area, or approach the District Labour Office.
- Migrant workers are entitled to these benefits regardless of their home state.
